About Penny

Penny (penny-experiences) is a single-owner private personal Chief of Staff, Priority Board, NYC Scout, and schedule planning web application operated solely by Darrell Crane.

Penny is not a public multi-user product or commercial SaaS service. All operational interfaces (/, /api/*) are strictly restricted to the application owner behind private authentication (WebAuthn Face ID platform passkey and rate-limited owner access token).

Core Capabilities

  • Priority Board: Reviews and triages the owner's personal Commitment Ledger stored in his private Open Brain Supabase database, with governed, version-checked commitment updates and 10-second undo.
  • NYC Scout: Evaluates curated NYC cultural and live-music events against the owner's personal schedule and taste profile without automated ticket purchasing.
  • Schedule Planning & Conflict Detection: Connects to the owner's personal Google Calendar to read busy/free windows in America/New_York time and to create new calendar blocks only when explicitly approved by the owner in the application.
