Single-Owner Privacy Policy

Application Privacy Policy

Penny (penny-experiences) is a single-owner private personal Chief of Staff, Priority Board, NYC Scout, and schedule planning application operated solely by Darrell Crane. It is restricted exclusively to the owner's personal use and does not accept public registrations or third-party user accounts.

1. Data Accessed

  • Commitment Ledger via Open Brain (yzmgvktiejiwrgsmthvj): Penny accesses only the owner's personal Commitment Ledger and durable experience preferences stored in his private Open Brain Supabase project (yzmgvktiejiwrgsmthvj).
  • Personal Google Calendar: With the owner's explicit OAuth authorization, Penny accesses only the owner's personal Google Calendar using:
    • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly (calendar.readonly) to read schedule windows in America/New_York time for schedule planning and conflict detection.
    • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events (calendar.events) strictly to create new calendar events that Darrell explicitly approves in the application, tagged with extendedProperties.private.penny = "1". Penny never edits, updates, or deletes existing Google Calendar events.

2. Data Storage & Ownership

All data accessed or generated by Penny stays strictly inside Darrell Crane's private Supabase (yzmgvktiejiwrgsmthvj) and Google Cloud (penny-personal-assistant) infrastructure. OAuth credentials are stored exclusively in Google Cloud Secret Manager and are never exposed to client browsers or logs.

3. No Sharing, Selling, Ads, or Analytics

Personal data and Google user data accessed by Penny are never shared with, sold to, or transferred to third parties. Data is never used for serving advertisements, and the application contains no ads, no tracking pixels, and no third-party analytics scripts.

4. Revocation & Contact

Google Calendar access can be revoked at any time via Google Account permissions at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions or by contacting the owner/developer at darrell.k.crane@gmail.com.
